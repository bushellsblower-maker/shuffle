How to play
  • Pull back anywhere and release to slide. Drag sideways to angle the shot. Touch low on the table to pick your lane. Or flick forward.
  • Sides alternate, 4 weights each. A weight must be fully past the red foul line or it's removed. Gutters are out.
  • Zones score 1–4. A hanger over the far edge scores +1 on top of its zone.
  • Only the side with the furthest weight scores: every one of its weights beyond the opponent's best.
  • The side that scored throws first next round. First to the target wins.
  • Ends switch every round: you shoot from the other end, at the other end's zones. The sand helps weights glide and nudges every slide a little.
  • Online: host a room and send the 5-letter code or link. Each phone throws its own weights; the room keeps the score.
